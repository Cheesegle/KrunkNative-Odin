kemar_diffuse.bin is a verbatim repacking of the MIT KEMAR diffuse-field
equalized HRTF set (128-point responses, 44.1 kHz, elevations -40..90 degrees),
produced with tools/hrtf_kemar_pack.py from
https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR/diffuse.zip

Authors: Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, MIT Media Lab Machine Listening Group.
Reference: W. G. Gardner and K. D. Martin, "HRTF measurements of a KEMAR",
J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 97(6), 1995, pp. 3907-3908.
